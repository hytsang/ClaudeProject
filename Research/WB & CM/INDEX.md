# Warren Buffett & Charlie Munger — Annual Letters to Shareholders

Source: <https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html> (official Berkshire Hathaway archive)

**Coverage:** 1977–2024 (48 letters)  
**Format:** Plain text (.txt), UTF-8  
**Folder:** `letters/`  

Each file is named `YYYY.txt`. Letters 1977–1997 were originally posted as HTML; letters 1998–2024 as PDF (this index already references the PDF-sourced text for those years). All letters are signed by Warren E. Buffett as Chairman; Charlie Munger appears as Vice Chairman throughout and contributed essays (notably the 50th-anniversary 2014 letter) until his death on November 28, 2023 (tributed in the 2023 letter).
